# Katechon

Katechon builds AI tools for making video stories with recurring characters, and uses them to run its own channels.

## Malfoid

**[malfoid.tv](https://malfoid.tv/?s=github)** is the home of **Malfoid**, our original AI-made series: rich, snobby, secretly soft. Season 1 premieres on Halloween, October 31, 2026. Short episodes run on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/katechon_studio/), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@katechon_studio_arc) and [X](https://x.com/Spooky_AGI).

## Products

**[Cast](https://cast.katechon.technology/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=org-profile)**: a mobile card game for short AI dramas, starring Malfoid & Friends. Open a free pack, watch the episode, then play a card to choose what happens next. It works in any phone browser with no sign-up.
- Deep links for AI assistants: `https://cast.katechon.technology/make?c=malfoid&p=<what happens>`
- Public no-auth MCP server: `https://cast.katechon.technology/mcp` (MCP Registry: `technology.katechon/cast`). Docs: [cast.katechon.technology/agents](https://cast.katechon.technology/agents)
- Flagship character: [Malfoid](https://cast.katechon.technology/characters/malfoid), with Thea and Odette (the free Malfoid & Friends pack)

**[Katechon Arc](https://arc.katechon.technology/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=org-profile)**: an AI studio for short dramas, films and video ads. It turns an idea, script or novel into characters, storyboards, video and voice. [Films made with Arc](https://arc.katechon.technology/films) · [MCP server](https://arc.katechon.technology/developers/mcp)

## Open data and guides (CC BY 4.0)

- [ai-video-price-index](https://github.com/Katechon-Technology/ai-video-price-index): dated USD-per-second list prices for 20 text-to-video models (Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan and more), CSV + JSON.
- [seedance-prompt-guide](https://github.com/Katechon-Technology/seedance-prompt-guide): a first-hand Seedance 2.0 Fast / 2.5 prompting field guide and the exact prompts behind published videos.
